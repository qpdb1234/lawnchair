<p><i>Lawnchair — AI Control Fork.</i> A personal fork of the open-source Lawnchair 15 (beta 3) home app that lets an <b>AI agent or a script drive your home screen</b>, plus a set of practical launcher tweaks. It installs and behaves like a normal Android home app; every extra power is an opt-in switch.</p><p><b>AI Control Interface (flagship):</b> nine independent, individually-toggleable entry points — Broadcast, Activity, Service, ContentProvider, Deep Link (<code>launcher://control?cmd=…</code>), batch insert, a loopback-only HTTP REST server on <code>localhost:9091</code>, MCP JSON-RPC, and dynamic launcher shortcuts — let an external agent or adb <b>list_icons, move_icon, add_icon, remove_icon and reload</b> the workspace. All channels are off by default and gated behind their own switch under Settings → AI control.</p><p><b>Also included:</b></p><ul><li><b>No-drawer home (toggle):</b> flatten every app onto the workspace instead of an app drawer, or re-enable the classic drawer with one switch; hide individual apps from home.</li><li><b>Classic drag mode:</b> no spring-loaded zoom or top bar; switch pages by hovering near the screen edge with a configurable trigger range.</li><li><b>Icon long-press menu:</b> choose which rows appear and in what order, plus a Force Stop entry.</li><li><b>Folder upgrades:</b> an add-apps editor with global de-duplication, a tight-fit grid, and a default folder tint.</li><li><b>In-app language switching</b> across 23 languages.</li><li>Log collection/export, reset settings to defaults, custom icon import, wallpaper blur, and experimental features promoted to stable.</li></ul><p><b>NOTE:</b> This is a community fork built on the official Lawnchair 15.0.0-beta3.0 tag. Lawnchair and its base project are made by the Lawnchair team — see the upstream project for the original app. Use at your own risk and back up your layout first.</p>
